Rigorous analysis of NEMO simulations requires consistent online and offline diagnostics of the heat, salt, and momentum budgets. We present a prototype of a community diagnostics package built on xarray and the online budget outputs of NEMO 5. The package provides closed budget terms, water mass transformation diagnostics, and ready-to-publish figures from a single configuration file. Use cases include the evaluation of the Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation in DRAKKAR runs and the comparison of mixing parameterizations.
